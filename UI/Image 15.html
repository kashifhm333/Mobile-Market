What happens if my phone's condition is different than quoted? expand_more
If our hardware diagnostic reveals a discrepant condition (e.g. undetected pixel damage, replaced non-genuine components, or battery below 80%), we will send an updated appraisal email with detailed high-res microscope photos. You have 7 days to accept the revised quote or reject it. If you reject it, we ship your phone back to you immediately with free return tracking. No questions asked.
How do I securely erase my personal data before sending? expand_more
For Apple iOS: Go to Settings > [Your Profile] > Sign Out of iCloud, then go to General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings. For Android: Remove your Google Account under Settings > Accounts, then proceed with a Factory Reset. In addition, our intake lab performs a DoD 5220.22-M certified military-grade cryptographic sanitization pass on every received phone before processing.
What if I change my mind after submitting the trade-in? expand_more
You are never under any contractual obligation. If you choose not to ship your device using the prepaid shipping kit, the quote simply expires after 14 days without any fee or penalty. Even if you've already sent it, you can request an instant cancellation prior to technician intake.
How does the 10% Store Credit bonus work? expand_more
When opting for NexPhone Exchange Store Credit rather than cash, we add an instant 10% bonus credit onto your valuation (e.g. a $500 cash trade-in becomes $550 store balance). This credit can be combined with existing coupon codes, used on any Brand New flagship or Certified Pre-Owned phone, and never expires.
Can I trade in a phone that is still on an installment plan or locked? expand_more
We accept carrier-locked devices from AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile! However, the device's IMEI must be clean (not flagged as lost, stolen, or associated with fraudulent account defaults). You remain responsible for any outstanding equipment installment plan balance with your respective cellular provider.
